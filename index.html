FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to make an order?

Production time depends on the size and complexity of the print. Most items will list an estimated production time before purchase.

Can I request a different color?

Yes. Available colors depend on the material currently in stock.

Do you accept custom STL files?

Yes. You can request a quote for an STL you already own or have permission to print.

Will 3D prints have layer lines?

Yes. FDM 3D printing creates products layer by layer, so minor layer lines and surface variations are normal.
